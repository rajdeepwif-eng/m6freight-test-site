What changes the price

  • Time: most local jobs have a 2-hour minimum; access, stairs and long carries add time.
  • People: a second person costs more per hour but often finishes faster.
  • Van size: bigger vans cost more per hour; picking the right one avoids a second trip.
  • Distance: long trips are usually priced per mile, including part of the empty drive back.
  • Day: weekends and same-day bookings usually cost more.

How we price

Local jobs: one all-in hourly rate (van, driver, fuel), no booking fee and no stairs fee. Longer trips: one fixed price before you book. See your price in seconds.

Tips to pay less

  • Pick a flexible date: we can fit you around trips we're already doing.
  • Have everything packed and by the door before we arrive.
  • Check parking at both ends; a long carry adds time.
  • Dismantle beds and tables before the van arrives.
