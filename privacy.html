Who we are

M6 FREIGHTUK LTD (company 17059854) is the controller of your personal data. Contact: info@m6freightuk.co.uk or 07301 519319.

What we collect and why

  • To price a job: the postcodes, items and date you type. They are sent to postcodes.io (a free UK postcode service) to work out distances. We don't need your name to show a price.
  • To carry out a booking: your name, mobile, email (optional), addresses and notes. Lawful basis: taking steps to enter into, and performing, a contract with you.
  • WhatsApp: when you tap a WhatsApp button, your booking text is sent through WhatsApp (Meta). We reply there. We only send updates if you ticked the box or messaged us, and you can reply STOP any time.
  • Payments: card payments are taken by Stripe on its own secure page. We never see your card number.

What this website stores

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not store your details in your browser. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
