Booking terms

Version: draft 2026-11-18

1. Prices

Local jobs (both addresses inside our local area) are charged by the hour at the rate shown before you book: van, driver and fuel included, no booking fee and no stairs fee. Weekend and same-day rates are shown in the price before you book. Minimum 2 hours, then 15-minute steps. The clock starts when we arrive at your collection address and stops when the job is done, including driving between your addresses. The website figure is an estimate; you pay for the actual time.

Trips (any other job) have one fixed all-in price for the items, addresses, access and date you gave us. If they're different on the day, we tell you the new price before loading and you can cancel instead.

Your booking is a request until we confirm it on WhatsApp. Local jobs: payment when the job is finished. Trips: a deposit holds the slot, taken on a secure Stripe page; the balance is due on completion. Trade accounts: invoice terms as agreed.
